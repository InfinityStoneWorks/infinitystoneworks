Before We Template

Homeowner Checklist

Five things to have ready before your template appointment — check them off and we'll be in and out in no time.

Download the checklist

PDF · one page · printable

Download PDF

  1. Sinks & faucets on site

    If you're supplying your own sink, it needs to be at the home by template day. Farm (apron) sinks must already be installed in their final position before our tech arrives.

  2. Cooktop or stove on site

    Along with your sink and faucet, we need your cooktop or stove — and downdraft, if you have one — on site. Still waiting on delivery? Send us the model number ahead of time.

  3. Cabinet install completed

    All cabinets for the project need to be fully installed and secured in their final locations before we'll template.

  4. Clear work space

    Templating over existing countertops instead of new cabinets? Clear the counters completely, including small appliances like the coffee maker.

  5. Decision maker on site & pets put away

    Someone who can make calls on backsplash height, edge detail, and similar details needs to be home during the final measurement. Please keep pets out of the work area while we're there.
