Frequently Asked Questions

Answers, before you have to ask.

Common questions about demo, veining, seams, and sink options — if yours isn't here, just reach out.

Don't see your question here? Reach us at Info@infinitystoneworks.net.

Does Infinity Stone Works Provide Demo?

Yes! We are able to complete demo and install on the same day. Demo is billed by the material and charged per square foot.

Can You Disconnect and Reconnect My Appliances?

Infinity Stone Works is not licensed to perform any plumbing or electrical work. All sink disconnections and reconnections, and any electrical work, needs to be completed by a licensed professional.

Will the Veins Match?

Yes. Our goal is to have 90% of all the veins matching. Our main focus is matching veins at seams, backsplash and waterfall panels. If you would like to see the vein layout, we can do a physical layout on your slabs after template, no additional charge. Digital 3D renderings are available for an additional charge.

Will I Be Able to See the Seams?

Yes. When gluing two pieces of stone together, there will inevitably be a seam in the material. Our seams are nearly invisible, which is above industry standard.

What Is a Better Option, an Undermount or Top-Mount Sink?

We always recommend using undermount sinks. Undermount sinks allow you to see more of your beautiful new countertops, and create ease when cleaning high-traffic areas in your home.

Can I Re-Use My Existing Sink?

You certainly can, however, we recommend replacing your sink.
